Real ball. Your swing.

A real-scale ball flies on drag, spin and wind, bites on the green and kicks off the trees. The timing is all yours.

◎

The swing meter

Click for power, click again on the strike bar. Hit the sweet spot and the golfer unloads in slow-motion; miss it and you'll feel it.

↺

Shape every shot

F and G walk the ladder: slight draw, draw, fade, slight fade, flop and punch. Bend it round the dogleg or knock it under the wind.

⚑

Read the green

Full-power, standard and close-touch putts. A higher WIS shows the line longer; the cup takes it under the right pace.

Two courses. Zero mercy.

Grudge Links is wind-scoured links golf: pot bunkers, deep rough, greens with real swales. Lake Drive bends its fairways along the water, past the homes on the shore.

  • Striped fairways, first cut, deep rough. Every lie plays differently
  • Trees that swat the ball and lakes that swallow it
  • Golf carts and a cart tour of every hole

The 19th hole is open.

Between rounds the clubhouse is the hangout: a game of pool, a putting mat contest, the pro shop wall of drivers, and range coaches who'll pick your swing apart.

  • Invite friends into your group and tee off together
  • A social tab that shows who's on the course
  • Level up, earn gold, kit out your bag
PvP tournament
